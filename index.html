              The AWS build, ready for your account

              One SAM template creates everything in the Petropolis AWS account. It validates clean, and the Lambda code is in the kit with its tests.

              Comes in

              • Goose webhooks: bookings, changes, check-in and check-out started
              • Goose poll every 2 minutes, in case a webhook is missed
              • Slack taps from phones, signed and checked
              • Managers sign in to the admin (Cognito)
              • Clock: 5:00 AM day plan, a tick every minute

              Decides

              • Slack ingress answers inside 3 seconds and queues the work
              • Worker applies taps, updates cards, sends the next task
              • Tick re-scores the queue and escalates overdue pet care
              • Day planner builds tasks and posts the coverage check
              • Offline list page and sync for weak signal

              Keeps

              • DynamoDB: tasks, shifts, staff, reservations; point-in-time recovery plus a daily backup
              • S3: task photos, copied out of Slack
              • Secrets Manager: Slack and Goose keys
              • CloudWatch alarms email you when something breaks

              Facts that shape the build

                Measured

                  Not measured

                  • Not connected to a real Slack workspace, Goose account or AWS account. The template is validated, not deployed.
                  • Goose’s real payload and webhook names are not public; the sample feed is Goose-shaped and invented.
                  • Lambda cold starts and real network time to Slack. The ack timing above is local Node.
                  • The offline page on real phones with real dead zones.
                  • Staff, shifts, task durations and incidents in the sample day are invented. Results show how the rules behave, not how Petropolis will perform.
                  • Costs are estimates from list prices and stated volumes.
